I am a second-year PhD student at [LIPADE](https://lipade.mi.parisdescartes.fr/), [Université Paris Cité](https://u-paris.fr/en/), advised by Professor [Themis Palpanas](https://helios2.mi.parisdescartes.fr/~themisp/), broadly interested in vector data management.

I have a MSc in computer science from [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/) in Switzerland, and a BSc in computer science from the [University of Crete](https://www.csd.uoc.gr/CSD/index.jsp?lang=en) in Greece, where I graduated first in my class.

I have worked at [Google](https://google.com) in the United States, the [Commision of Atomic Energy (CEA)](https://www.cea.fr/) in France, [DIAS EPFL lab](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/dias/) in Switzerland, and [FORTH-ICS](https://www.forth.gr/en/home/) in Greece.

I am a doctoral scholar of the [Onassis Foundation](https://www.onassis.org), and an graduate scholar of the [Latsis Foundation](https://www.latsis-foundation.org/eng) and the [Onassis Foundation](https://www.onassis.org). 
I am also honored to be an alumni of the [9th Heidelberg Laureate Forum](https://www.heidelberg-laureate-forum.org/forum/9th-hlf-2022.html).

For more details, please see [curriculum vitae](https://mchatzakis.github.io/assets/pdf/Chatzakis_CV.pdf).
