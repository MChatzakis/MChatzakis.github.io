Manos Chatzakis

PhD Student @ LIPADE, Université Paris Cité | Ex-Google, EPFL, CEA, UoC

705G, 45 Rue des Saints-Pères, 75006 Paris, France

I am a second-year PhD student at LIPADE, Université Paris Cité, advised by Professor Themis Palpanas, broadly interested in vector data management.

I have obtained my MSc in computer science from EPFL in Switzerland, and my BSc in computer science from the University of Crete in Greece, where I graduated first in my class.

I have worked at Google in the United States, the Commision of Atomic Energy (CEA) in France, DIAS EPFL lab in Switzerland, and FORTH-ICS in Greece.

I am a doctoral scholar of the Onassis Foundation, and an graduate scholar of the Latsis Foundation and the Onassis Foundation. I am also honored to be an alumni of the 9th Heidelberg Laureate Forum.

For more details, please see curriculum vitae.
